How each role is scored (v2, 0–100)

Target
Only Summer 2027 internships are ranked. Full-time, new-grad and off-cycle roles (fall, spring, co-op, part-time) are kept but sit in Tier D.
Value level · 70 / 40 / 0
Top: AI PM, or a role you marked 👍. Standard: data analysis / BI, PM (incl. product ops and solutions), data science, AI roles. Excluded: unrelated fields, hardware, pure machine-learning engineering (client-facing AI engineering stays Standard), or a role you marked 👎. Three levels only, because two blind checks (Nov 3) showed your preferences inside the eligible pool are close to flat.
Chance · up to 30
30 × (0.40 referral + 0.25 timing + 0.20 sponsorship + 0.15 freshness). Referral: 1st-degree 100%, LinkedIn connection 80%, warm intro 60%, alumni 40%, cold 0. Timing: "Summer 2027" in the posting 100%, "summer" or "2027" 80%, not stated 50%. Sponsorship: posting offers it 100%, strong H-1B sponsor 80%, some history 60%, unknown 40%. Freshness: posted ≤ 7 days before we found it 100% … over 90 days 20%. These weights are starting priors; they are refitted from your tracker outcomes as replies come in.
Eligibility (full JD check)
Every posting's full job description is read from its job board and checked against your situation; Not eligible / Closed / Duplicate postings and postings that rule out sponsorship are Blocked.
Tiers
A = Top level · B = Standard with chance ≥ 50% · C = Standard below 50% · D = Excluded or not a Summer 2027 internship · Blocked. Location is never penalized.
Your 👍 / 👎
Click 👍 or 👎 on a role (the "You" column or the detail panel; click again to clear). The local server scripts/scoring/pref_server.py (LaunchAgent com.jobagent.pref, 127.0.0.1:8766) re-scores the role at once; the page shows the new level and tier, and the dashboard rebuild follows within 60 s. If the server is down the button says so. Chat or scripts/scoring/pref.py <id> up|down|clear still work; every mark is kept in data/scoring/prefs.jsonl as training data for a later preference model.
Caveats
H-1B data counts DOL labor condition applications and is matched to companies by name; a few large employers are still mis-matched (JSA-098).
