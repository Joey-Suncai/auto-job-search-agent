# JSA-238 — internships and "no sponsorship"; co-ops without dates (Joey 2026-11-06)

The candidate is an F-1 international master's student. A summer internship runs on CPT (Curricular Practical Training),
which needs no employer visa sponsorship. Full-time roles would need sponsorship (OPT, then H-1B).

Verdicts: Eligible, Review (a person reads the posting), Ineligible.

1. Internship (level Internship, or "intern"/"internship"/"co-op" in the title) whose posting only says the employer does not
   provide visa / immigration sponsorship (e.g. "we do not offer visa sponsorship for this position", "Sponsorship: Not
   Available", "no sponsorship for this program", "this role is open to candidates who do not need visa sponsorship")
   → Eligible: CPT covers the internship. Joey: 「如果只是说不提供担保，但是可以实习的话就不拦。」
2. Internship whose posting rules out the internship itself → Ineligible: F-1 / CPT / OPT / student visas are named as not
   accepted or not eligible (e.g. "unable to sponsor any visa, including F-1 (OPT/CPT)", "CPT/OPT candidates are not
   eligible"), or the candidate must be authorized to work "for any employer", or needs permanent / unrestricted U.S.
   work authorization. Joey: 「如果是实习都不能实习，就拦掉。」
3. Internship whose no-sponsorship sentence covers the future ("now or in the future", "currently or in the future",
   "at any time", "future sponsorship", "must not require sponsorship now or in the future") and does not name F-1 /
   CPT / OPT → Review (a return offer would need H-1B; Joey decides).
4. Full-time (non-intern) roles: any "no sponsorship" statement → Ineligible (unchanged).
5. A posting that also says the employer may sponsor / sponsors case by case → not a block (unchanged: Review or Eligible).
6. Co-op roles (co-op in the title, no "summer" in the title): if the posting states an off-summer term (fall / spring /
   winter 2026 or 2027, a fall/spring semester, a date range that never touches June–August, or an off-summer start month
   next to the co-op such as "starting in January") → Ineligible. A co-op whose posting gives no term or dates at all →
   Review (Joey: 「改成待核实」). A co-op in Summer 2027 → Eligible.
7. Words like "the future of mobility" are not about sponsorship. "Prior internship or co-op experience" describes the
   candidate, not the role.
