SPEC: answering "may we contact your employer?" questions for a candidate who is a full-time student with NO current job (past internships ended; he is not currently employed).
Rule 1: a question that is conditional on current employment ("If you are currently employed, may we contact your employer?", "If currently employed, may we contact your current supervisor?") is answered "N/A" in a text box, or with the option whose text means not applicable ("N/A", "Not applicable", "Does not apply") when such an option exists. It is never "Yes".
Rule 2: if the conditional question offers only Yes / No options, with no not-applicable option, no answer is given ("ask").
Rule 3: the plain, unconditional question "May we contact your current employer?" with Yes / No options keeps the candidate's earlier standing answer "Yes".
Rule 4: a question about previous employers ("May we contact your previous employers?") is not conditional on current employment and is answered "Yes".
Rule 5: option order must not matter.
