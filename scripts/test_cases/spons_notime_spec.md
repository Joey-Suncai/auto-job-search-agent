# Sponsorship questions with no time word are never answered automatically (JSA-361 stop-gap, Joey 2026-11-16)

Joey's decision (2026-11-16): a question about **needing visa / immigration sponsorship** (or being authorized to work
**without** sponsorship) that names **no time** is not answered by the rules — it always goes to Joey. His situation
differs by time (now: no sponsorship needed; later, full-time: needed), so without a time word the answer is a guess.

**A time word** is any of: now, currently, current, presently, at this time, today, immediately, future, ever, at any time
/ at any point, any time, later, eventually, going forward, upon graduation, after graduating, to begin / to start /
to commence (working, employment), for / during / in / to participate in this (or the) internship, the duration of the
internship, within the next N months / years, in the next N months / years.

**Must ask (no time word):**
- "Do you require Visa sponsorship?"
- "Do you require immigration sponsorship to work for a Greenhouse employer in the United States?"
- "Do you require sponsorship?"
- "Do you require visa sponsorship for employment in the USA?" (options may be long sentences)
- "Do you require visa sponsorship to work in the United States?"
- "Requesting visa sponsorship?"
- "Do you require employer-sponsored work authorization?"
- "Will you require visa sponsorship to work for an employer?" ("will" alone is not a time word)
- "If you answered 'No' to the previous question, will you require sponsorship for a work visa or other work authorization?"
- "Are you legally authorized to work in the US without sponsorship?"
- "Are you legally authorized to work in the country for this position without requiring employer-sponsored work authorization?"
- "Without sponsorship, are you legally permitted to work in the country where this job is located?"
