# Is this form question the yes/no "will you require visa sponsorship" question?

An answer audit checks one kind of question on job application forms: whether the applicant now or in the future will
require employer sponsorship for a work visa (expected answer for him: Yes). Given a question label, say whether it is
such a question (true) or not (false).
True: "Will you now or in the future require sponsorship for employment visa status (e.g., H-1B)?", "Do you require visa sponsorship?",
"Sponsorship required?".
False: long legal agreements or consent texts that only mention the word "sponsor" in another sense (an arbitration agreement
covering "the Company's benefit plans or the plans' sponsors", "plan sponsors", "event sponsors", "executive sponsor"),
questions about the TYPE or category of sponsorship, "check all that apply" lists, relocation support, and questions that do not
mention sponsorship.
