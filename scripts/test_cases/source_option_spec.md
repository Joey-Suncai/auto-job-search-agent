# May the agent pick this "How did you hear about us?" option?

The candidate found every job on public job boards, company career sites or aggregator lists. He was never referred,
never contacted by the company or a recruiter, never met the company at a campus event, career fair, conference or
information session, never worked there in any form (employee, temporary, contingent, contractor), and was not placed by
an agency or staffing firm. A rule picks the first option of the source dropdown that does NOT make such a claim.
Return true when the option is allowed (a channel he could truthfully have used: a website, job board, social network,
search engine, advertisement, publication), false when it claims a person, contact, event, school, program or prior work
(e.g. "Employee Referral", "Referred by a Recruiter", "Company Contacted Me", "Campus Recruiting Event", "Career Fair",
"Auburn University", "Current Temporary Employee", "Agency / Search Firm", "Staffing Company", "Alumni Network",
"In-Person", a named recruiting program).
Empty option → false.
