# Choosing the master's degree option on a Workday education block

The candidate is studying for a Master of Science (M.S. in Applied Data Science). A Workday "Degree" dropdown lists
degree options. From the list, choose the option for his master's:
1. Never choose a specialty master's that is not his: law (e.g. "MS-Law", "Master of Laws", "LLM", "JD"), executive
   ("Executive Master", "EMBA"), business administration only ("MBA"), education ("M.Ed"), fine arts ("MFA"), social
   work ("MSW"), divinity, nursing, public health / administration / policy ("MPH", "MPA", "MPP"), architecture, music,
   pharmacy, engineering-only ("Master of Engineering", "M.Eng"), or technology ("Master of Technology").
   An option that names Science together with another field ("Master of Science or Engineering",
   "Masters of Arts or Science or Business Administration (Masters)") is allowed.
2. Among the allowed master's options, prefer: "Master of Science" / "Master's of Science" / "M.S." / "MS" / "MSc"
   first; then a plain "Master's Degree" / "Masters" / "Master's"; then any other allowed master's option.
3. Ties keep the list order. No allowed master's option → None.
