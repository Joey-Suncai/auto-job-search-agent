# Graduation requirement tied to a bachelor's degree

The candidate already holds a B.A. (May 2026) and is now a master's student graduating December 2027. A job-eligibility
checker must mark REVIEW (a human reads it) when the JD says the candidate must graduate with / be completing a bachelor's
degree in the window — e.g. "Students expecting to graduate in December 2027 through June 2028 with a bachelor's degree",
"graduating with a BS in 2028", "expected to graduate with an undergraduate degree by May 2028".
It must NOT mark this review when the JD accepts a master's too ("graduating with a bachelor's or master's degree",
"bachelor's or master's students graduating Dec 2027 – Jun 2028"), or when it only mentions a bachelor's as a minimum
("Bachelor's degree required" for full-time roles), or never mentions graduation.
Answer: true when REVIEW (BACHELORS_WORDING) must be reported, false otherwise.
