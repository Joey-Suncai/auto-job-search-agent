# Which audited education question is this form label?

An answer audit checks two education questions about the applicant's COLLEGE education:
- "school": the label asks for the name of the college / university he attends or attended (expected: University of Chicago or
  University of Illinois). Example: "School or University", "Institution name".
- "degree_level": the label asks for a college degree or level he holds or is earning (expected: Masters / Bachelors).
  Examples: "Degree", "Highest level of education", "My highest degree completed beyond high school is:", "Highest degree earned".
- "other": anything else — in particular every question about his HIGH SCHOOL (its name, its location, the high-school diploma,
  the year he finished high school), and questions that are yes/no or about dates, GPA, location of a school, or employment.
Given a label, say which one it is.
