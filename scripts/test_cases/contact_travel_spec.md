# Two fixed application-form answers (Joey 2026-11-01)

An answer-rule engine maps a form question (label, field type, options) to an answer.
- "May we contact your current employer?" (or present / previous / former employer, supervisor or manager; "Can we
  contact …") → "Yes".
- A question asking whether he would require / need travel assistance, travel reimbursement or travel support to attend
  an interview (in person, on site, at an office) → "No".
These must not change other questions:
- "Current employer" / "Most recent employer" as a text box → "University of Chicago".
- "Are you willing to travel up to 25% for this role?" → "Yes".
- "Are you able to work in person in our Chicago office?" → "Yes".
Questions outside these patterns are not part of this test; do not include them.
