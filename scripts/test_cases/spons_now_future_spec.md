# Sponsorship questions: "now" vs "future" (candidate: F-1 student, summer internship on CPT, needs H-1B for full-time)

Facts: sponsorship NOW = No (the internship runs on CPT). Sponsorship in the FUTURE (after graduation / full-time / at any time) = Yes.

A Yes/No sponsorship question is classified as one of:
- "future" (answer Yes): any question that covers the future or any time after graduation, even if it also says "now".
  Examples of future wording: "now or in the future", "now or at any time after graduation", "at any time", "upon graduation",
  "later", "eventually", "going forward", "in the future", "will you require … for full-time".
  A question with no time word at all ("Will you require visa sponsorship?") is also "future" (the safe, true answer is Yes).
- "now" (answer No): the question asks only about the present: "now", "currently", "at this time", "to begin this internship",
  placed anywhere in the sentence ("Do you now require sponsorship", "Do you require sponsorship at this time?"),
  and has no future wording.
Not a sponsorship Yes/No question (classify "other"): "What type of sponsorship do you need?", "Which visa type?",
"Are you legally authorized to work in the US?" (work authorization, not sponsorship), "When do you graduate?".
