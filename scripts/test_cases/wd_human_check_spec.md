# Recognising a "verify you are human" question on an application form (2026-11-03)
Predicate is_human_check(label) -> bool. Such questions (and CAPTCHAs) are always answered by the candidate himself, never by a model or a rule. True when the label asks the applicant to prove they are a human: "verify (that) you are (a) human", "are you a human" / "are you a robot", "not a robot", mentions CAPTCHA, or "prove you are (a) human" (up to ~20 characters between "prove you" and "human"). Case-insensitive. Examples that are true: "Please verify you are human, count the number of t in eeootteotto.", "I'm not a robot", "CAPTCHA: type the letters". False for ordinary questions that merely mention humans or verification: "Can you provide verification of your identity?", "Human Resources contact", "Are you able to verify your employment eligibility?", "Do you have experience with human-centered design?", "Robotics experience".
