# Degree-level answer audit

The candidate holds a B.A. in Economics (UIUC, 2026) and is studying for an M.S. in Applied Data Science (UChicago,
expected Dec 2027). A checker reads the value recorded in a "Degree" field of an education block and says whether it is
an acceptable description of one of his two degrees (true) or wrong (false). It does not know which block it is.
True: "Masters", "Master's", "Master's Degree", "Master of Science", "M.S.", "MS", "Masters of Arts or Science or
Business Administration (Masters)", "Bachelors", "Bachelor's Degree", "Bachelor of Arts", "B.A.", "BA",
"Post Graduate Degree", "Postgraduate", "Graduate", "Graduate Degree", "Undergraduate", "Undergraduate Degree".
False: any Doctorate / PhD / Juris Doctor, "Associate", "MBA" / "Master of Business Administration",
"Master of Technology", "High School", "Diploma", "Certificate", an empty value.
Capitalisation does not matter.
