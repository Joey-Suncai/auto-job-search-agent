# Sponsorship answer audit with statement options

The candidate is an F-1 student who WILL require visa sponsorship (now or in the future). A checker reads the answer
recorded for a sponsorship question ("Will you now or in the future require sponsorship…?") and says whether it is
truthful for him (true) or wrong (false).
True: "Yes", "Yes, I will require sponsorship", and statement options that say he requires / needs / will require
sponsorship, e.g. "I require sponsorship to work in this country", "I will require visa sponsorship", "I would need H-1B
sponsorship", "I need employer sponsorship".
False: "No", "I do not require sponsorship", "I will not need sponsorship", "I never need sponsorship", "I am a U.S.
citizen", "I am authorized to work without sponsorship", "No, I do not require sponsorship", an empty answer.
Capitalisation does not matter.
