# School question or field-of-study question? And the start-availability answer

1. A form label that asks WHICH SCHOOL the candidate attends is a school question, even when it also mentions the
   major: "What school are you currently attending for your major?", "Which university do you attend?",
   "School", "University name", "What college are you attending?". Answer: the school ("University of Chicago").
2. A label that asks for the field of study is a discipline question: "Major", "Field of study", "Discipline",
   "What is your major?", "Intended major". Answer: the field ("Data Science" or the closest option).
3. "When are you available to start an internship?" — in a DATE box the answer is the date "06/14/2027"; in a text box
   or a select it is the season "Summer 2027" (or the option that starts with "Summer").
Output for each label: which rule answers it — "school", "discipline" or "avail_season" — and the value given the field
type and options.
