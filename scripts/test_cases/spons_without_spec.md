# Spec: answering "work WITHOUT sponsorship" questions (form-filling rules)

Candidate facts (from the profile's standard answers):
- F-1 international student. The Summer 2027 internship is done on CPT.
- "Do you require sponsorship NOW?" → No (the internship is on CPT).
- "Will you require sponsorship in the FUTURE?" → Yes (OPT/STEM OPT then H-1B for full-time).

Rule:
1. A question that asks whether he can / is authorized / is eligible to work WITHOUT sponsorship is the opposite of
   "do you require sponsorship", so the answer is flipped.
2. If the question covers the future or the time after the internship — words such as "future", "now or in the future",
   "after you graduate", "upon graduation", "at any time", "later", "eventually", "going forward", "long-term",
   "full-time employment" — the future answer applies: requires sponsorship in the future = Yes → "able to work without
   sponsorship" = **No**.
3. If it covers only now, the internship, "this position", "at this time", "currently", "for the duration of this
   internship" (no future words) → the now answer applies: requires sponsorship now = No → "able to work without
   sponsorship" = **Yes**.
4. A question that asks whether he REQUIRES / NEEDS sponsorship (the word require/need comes before "without", or there
   is no "without") is not flipped: "Will you now or in the future require sponsorship?" → Yes; "Do you currently require
   sponsorship?" → No.
5. Options are Yes / No; the answer is the option text.
