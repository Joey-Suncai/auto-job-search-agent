SPEC: answering form questions about work-visa sponsorship for a candidate who does NOT need sponsorship now (on CPT as a student) but WILL need it in the future (H-1B).
A form question may offer several options. When the question asks "now or in the future" and the options are split by time — e.g. "Yes, I will require sponsorship now …", "Yes, I will require sponsorship in the future …", "No, I do not and will not require sponsorship …" — the answer is the option that says Yes for the FUTURE only. Never the "now" Yes option, never the No option.
If the options are one Yes ("Yes, I will require sponsorship now or in the future") and one No, the answer is that Yes option. If the question is a plain Yes/No asking only about NOW ("Do you currently require sponsorship?") the answer is "No". If several Yes options exist and none of them is clearly the future-only one, no answer is given (status asks the user).
Option texts vary in wording and order; the option order must not matter.
