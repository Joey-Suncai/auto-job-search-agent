# Veteran-status answer check

The candidate has never served in any military. A checker reads the answer recorded for a "Veteran status" question on a
job application and says whether it is truthful for him (true) or wrong (false).

True (acceptable): any answer that says he is not a veteran or not a protected veteran, including tenant wording such as
"No", "I am not a veteran", "I am not a protected veteran", "I am not a Veteran (United States of America)",
"I am not a Veteran (U.S.)", "I am not a Veteran (I did not serve in the military).", "I have never served in the military"
(a "never served" phrasing).

False (wrong for him): any answer that says he IS a veteran of some kind — "I identify as a Veteran, just not a protected
Veteran", "I identify as one or more of the classifications of protected veteran", "Recently separated veteran",
"Active duty wartime or campaign badge veteran", "Disabled veteran", "Armed Forces service medal veteran", "Yes" — and
"I don't wish to answer" / "Decline to self-identify" (he answers this question), and an empty answer.
Capitalisation and a trailing period do not matter.

Also true (2026-11-01, Arrowstreet): quoted tenant wording such as 'I am NOT a "Protected Veteran" as described above' or
"I am not a 'protected veteran' as defined above". Also false: 'I identify as a "Protected Veteran" as described above'.
Also true: options that begin with "No," — "No, I am not a protected veteran.", "No, I am not a veteran".
Also false: "Yes, I identify as belonging to one or more of the classifications of protected veterans listed above."
Also true: "Not a Veteran", "Not a Protected Veteran". Also false: "Veteran".
Also true: "No, I do not believe one or more of the above categories apply to me." Also false: "Yes, I believe one or more of the above categories apply to me."
Also true: "Not Applicable" (when the options are veteran categories).
