# Citizen / permanent-resident status inside a work-authorization question (JSA-310, KBR audit 2026-11-11)

Candidate: F-1 student, citizen of China only; NOT a U.S. citizen, national, permanent resident or green-card holder.
His rule for work-authorization questions: answer Yes ("Are you legally authorized to work in the U.S.?", "for any
employer", "without restriction", "as of today" → Yes). Exception: when the question asks whether he is authorized
AS a U.S. citizen / national / (lawful) permanent resident / green-card holder — i.e. it asks for that status — the
answer is No. Citizen / permanent resident named only as an EXAMPLE of authorization ("(e.g., U.S. citizen, permanent
resident, F-1 OPT)", "such as a U.S. citizen or …") does not make it a status question → Yes. "Are you a U.S. citizen or
permanent resident?" → No. Sponsorship questions: "Will you now or in the future require sponsorship?" → Yes.
