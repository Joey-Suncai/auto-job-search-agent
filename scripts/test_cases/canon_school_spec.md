# Is this job-application form label the question "which school / university do you attend?" (JSA-321)

The candidate's high-stakes check compares the answer of THE SCHOOL QUESTION with his real schools. A label counts as
the school question only when it asks for the school itself: the name of the school / college / university /
institution the applicant attends or attended, as a bare field name ("School", "University", "School or University",
"College/University", "School name", "Name of school") or as a request or question ("Enter your university", "Please
select your current school from the list", "the university you currently attend", "Which college do you attend?",
"What university are you currently enrolled with?", "If your school is not listed above, please enter the school name",
"If other, enter school / institution name").

It is NOT the school question when the label mentions a school but asks something else: the grading scale or GPA used by
the school, grades, graduation date or year, the school's e-mail address, location, city, state or country, a high
school, whether the applicant is enrolled or will return to school, a degree / major / field of study, an employer or a
public institution, or an essay ("Describe a project from school", "Tell us about your university experience").

Labels are given lower-case or mixed case and may end with ':' '*' or '?'.
