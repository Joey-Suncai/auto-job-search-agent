SPEC: answering job-form questions for a candidate who lives in Chicago, is willing to relocate (the answer to "are you willing to relocate?" is Yes), works hybrid/on site happily (Yes), and needs NO accommodation of any kind (answer to "do you require reasonable adjustments / accommodations to take part in the recruitment process / interview" is No).
Rule 1: a question that asks whether the candidate requires accommodations or adjustments for the application process is answered "No", even when its text lists examples (extra time on tests, wheelchair access in the office, sign-language interpreter) or an exclusion note ("Do not include VISA or relocation expectations", "(excluding travel)").
Rule 2: words that appear only inside an exclusion note ("do not include X", "excluding X", "other than X") are NOT part of the question: a question that says "Do not include relocation" is never a relocation question; "Do not include remote work" is never a work-arrangement question.
Rule 3: a genuine relocation question ("Are you willing to relocate for this role?") is still answered "Yes"; a genuine office-attendance question ("Are you willing to work on site 4 days a week?") is still answered "Yes". A housing question ("Do you need accommodation (housing) during the internship?") is NOT this rule: it must not be answered by it (expect "ask").
