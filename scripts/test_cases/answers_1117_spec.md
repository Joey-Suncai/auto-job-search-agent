SPEC: answering job-form questions for a candidate with these confirmed facts:
- He has NOT completed the Options 101 online course of a trading firm: the answer to "Did you complete our online Options 101 Course?" is the option that says "Not yet!" (options may be: "Not yet!", "I'm >50% through the online Options 101 course", "I have concluded the online Options 101 course"). Same for any "did you complete our online <X> course?" question: the "not yet"/"No" option.
- He has never held a visitor visa (B-1/B-2): a question "Visitor Visa" (Yes/No) or "Have you ever held a B-1/B-2 visitor visa?" is answered "No".
- He is a master's student graduating December 2027. "Will you be enrolled in the fall semester following the internship?" (a Summer 2027 internship) is "Yes". (A question about Fall 2028 is not covered.)
- Which other cities he would relocate to (multi-select): only United States and China cities: Chicago, New York, San Francisco, Boston, Shanghai, Beijing, Hong Kong ... are picked; London, Bristol, Amsterdam, Paris, Singapore, Mumbai, Sydney, Toronto, Tokyo are NOT picked. The answer is the picked options joined with " | " in the order of the option list. If no listed city qualifies, no answer is given ("ask").
- A required recorded video (record a video / Loom / screencast / video introduction): no answer is given ("ask"). A text box asking for a "Portfolio / video resume URL" is not a recorded video and keeps its normal answer (a URL) — do not include it in cases.
- EEO: "Do you wish to furnish this information?" with options No / Yes is answered "Yes".
- "What is your current education level?" (options like High School, Undergraduate, Master's, PhD) is "Master's". "What is your highest level of education?" is the completed one: a bachelor's — not part of these cases.
- "Enter your college/university" (text) → the candidate's university (starts with "University of"). A bare "High School" text box → the high school on file (ends with "High School"). A "which US state do you reside in?" question → "Illinois".
- Not covered, must ask: "Year you expect to begin full time employment", "Recruiting Timelines", a box just named "Extra".
