# transportation rule
Fact: the candidate does NOT have reliable transportation (no car). He DOES hold a valid Illinois driver's license (a
different question). Classify a Yes/No question: "transport" when it asks whether he has reliable transportation / a
reliable means of transportation / his own car or vehicle to get to work (expected answer "No"); "other" for driver's
license questions, willingness to travel, commute distance, relocation, or anything else.
