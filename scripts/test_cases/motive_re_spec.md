# Is a form question a "why this job / company" question?

Joey's rule (2026-10-31): a question asking why the candidate wants THIS job or THIS company is always answered, even
when optional. Given the question label (any case), answer true when it asks for the candidate's motivation or interest
in the role / company / team / position, in any tense, for example "Why do you want to join X?", "What interested you
in X and/or this position?", "What excites you about this role?", "What drew you to X?", "Why are you applying?",
"What motivates you to pursue this opportunity?", "Tell us why you are interested in this internship".
Answer false for questions that are not about motivation: location, dates, visa, salary, "How did you hear about us?",
"Are you interested in our newsletter / talent community / Women's program?" (a yes/no opt-in, not motivation),
"What is the most interesting paper you read?", "Which department are you most interested in?" (a choice, not a why),
"Describe a project you are proud of".
