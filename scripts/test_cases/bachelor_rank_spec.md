# Choosing the bachelor's degree option on a Workday education block

The candidate holds a Bachelor of Arts in Economics (B.A., University of Illinois). A Workday "Degree" dropdown lists
degree options. Choose the option for his bachelor's degree, or None when no option fits:
1. Never choose a different bachelor's: Bachelor of Science (B.S., BSc) alone, Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA), music,
   architecture, nursing (BSN), education (B.Ed), engineering (B.E., B.Eng), social work (BSW), business administration
   (BBA), pharmacy, technology, law (LLB), divinity, applied science.
2. Prefer "Bachelor of Arts" / "Bachelor's of Arts" / "B.A." / "BA" first; then a plain "Bachelor's Degree" /
   "Bachelors" / "Bachelor's" / "Baccalaureate"; then an option that combines Arts with another degree
   ("Bachelor of Arts or Science").
3. Ties keep the list order. Master's, associate, doctorate and high-school options are never chosen.
