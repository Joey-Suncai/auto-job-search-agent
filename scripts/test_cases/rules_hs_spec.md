# hs_diploma rule
Fact: the candidate has a high school diploma (graduated 2022). A Yes/No question asking whether he HAS / holds /
completed / earned a high school diploma, high school degree, GED, or "high school diploma or equivalent", or secondary
school diploma → "Yes". Questions asking WHEN / which year / the date / the NAME or location of the school are NOT this
rule → "none". Unrelated questions → "none".
