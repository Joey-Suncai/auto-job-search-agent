# Immigration / work-authorization status answer audit

The candidate is an F-1 student who will need sponsorship. A checker reads the answer recorded for a question like
"What is your current immigration status?" / "What is your work authorization status?" and says whether it is truthful.
True: "F-1", "F1", "F-1 Student", and statement options that say he requires / needs / will require sponsorship
("I require sponsorship to work in this country", "I will require visa sponsorship", "I need employer sponsorship").
False: "U.S. Citizen", "Permanent Resident", "Green Card", "H-1B", "J-1", "OPT", "F-1 CPT" / "CPT" (his saved rule: plain
F-1, not CPT), "I am authorized to work without sponsorship", "I do not require sponsorship", an empty answer.
